# Bijoux

**It packs your suitcase from your own closet.**

An AI wardrobe. Photograph the clothes you already own and they are tagged
automatically by vision AI. Bijoux then acts as a personal stylist —
recommending outfits from _your_ wardrobe, adjusted to the real weather
outside, and packing a suitcase for a trip.
